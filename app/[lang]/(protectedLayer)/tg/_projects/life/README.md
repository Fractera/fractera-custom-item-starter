# Life (Жизнь) — a section of Projects

A STATIC folder: its groups below are folders; a new group = a folder with meta.json, README.md and items.json, then `npm run tg:projects` and a build.

- `self` — Self-improvement: Health, body, mind and habits: everything that keeps you in shape.
- `finance` — Personal finance and investing: Your money: spending, savings, investing and payments.
- `family` — Family and relationships: Dates, gifts, children and shared plans.
- `home` — Home and smart home: Your home, its devices, repairs and daily life.
- `food` — Food and recipes: Menus, recipes, shopping and diet.
- `travel` — Travel and relocation: Routes, stays, documents and trip budgets.
- `car` — Car and transport: Service, costs and everything about your car.
- `pets` — Pets: Health, care and shopping for your pets.
- `hobby` — Creativity and hobbies: What you do for the soul.
- `media` — Films, books and music: What to watch, read and hear, and what you think of it.
- `games` — Games: What to play, how to beat it and with whom.
