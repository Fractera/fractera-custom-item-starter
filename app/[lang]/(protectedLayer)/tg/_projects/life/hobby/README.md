# Creativity and hobbies (Творчество и хобби) — `projects/hobby`

What you do for the soul.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `music` (Music and instruments), `art` (Drawing, photo and design), `diy` (Crafts and DIY), `collecting` (Collecting). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "hobby/<item>"` (or `"hobby"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** music, films and books you consume → `media`; games → `games`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
