# Family and relationships (Семья и отношения) — `projects/family`

Dates, gifts, children and shared plans.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `dates` (Important dates and gift ideas), `household` (Family budget and chores), `kids` (Children and activities), `plans` (Shared plans and leisure). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "family/<item>"` (or `"family"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** the home itself → `home`; trips together → `travel`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
