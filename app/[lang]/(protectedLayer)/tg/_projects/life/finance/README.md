# Personal finance and investing (Личные финансы и инвестиции) — `projects/finance`

Your money: spending, savings, investing and payments.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `budget` (Budget and spending), `savings` (Savings and goals), `investing` (Investing, stocks and crypto), `payments` (Loans and regular payments). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "finance/<item>"` (or `"finance"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** the business's money → `business/economy`; crypto → `web3`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
