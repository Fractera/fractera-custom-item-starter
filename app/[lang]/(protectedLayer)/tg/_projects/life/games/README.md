# Games (Игры и гейминг) — `projects/games`

What to play, how to beat it and with whom.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `backlog` (Backlog), `guides` (Walkthroughs, guides and builds), `esports` (Esports and tournaments), `squad` (Streams, clans and teammates), `hardware` (Hardware and consoles), `gamedev` (Game dev and mods). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "games/<item>"` (or `"games"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** films and books → `media`; hardware of the home → `home`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
