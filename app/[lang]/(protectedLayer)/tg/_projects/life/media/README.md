# Films, books and music (Кино, книги и музыка) — `projects/media`

What to watch, read and hear, and what you think of it.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `films` (Films and series), `books` (Books and notes), `music` (Music and podcasts), `ratings` (Recommendations and ratings). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "media/<item>"` (or `"media"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** your own creative work → `hobby`; content you publish → `audience/content`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
