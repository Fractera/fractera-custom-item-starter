# Self-improvement (Самосовершенствование) — `projects/self`

Health, body, mind and habits: everything that keeps you in shape.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `health` (Health), `psychology` (Psychology), `sport` (Sport), `habits` (Habits and routine), `learning` (Learning and skills). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "self/<item>"` (or `"self"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** study for a career → `career`; food and diet → `food`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
