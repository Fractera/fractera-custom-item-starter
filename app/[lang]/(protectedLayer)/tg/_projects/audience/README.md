# Voice and audience (Голос и аудитория) — a section of Projects

A STATIC folder: its groups below are folders; a new group = a folder with meta.json, README.md and items.json, then `npm run tg:projects` and a build.

- `social` — Social networks: Your channels and what you publish there.
- `platform` — Platform: The places where your texts and site live.
- `content` — Content: Everything your publications are made of.
- `mailings` — Mailings: Letters and messages that warm people up and bring them back.
- `marketing` — Marketing and traffic: Where people come from and what it costs.
- `brand` — Brand and packaging: How people recognise you and how you sound.
- `community` — Community and networking: The people around you: clubs, contacts, partners.
- `events` — Events: Broadcasts, meetings and reasons to be heard.
- `watch` — Watchtower: News and trends of your niche while they are fresh.
