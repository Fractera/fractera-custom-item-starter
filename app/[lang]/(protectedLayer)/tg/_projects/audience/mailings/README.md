# Mailings (Рассылки) — `projects/mailings`

Letters and messages that warm people up and bring them back.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `email` (Email), `whatsapp` (WhatsApp), `telegram` (Telegram), `funnels` (Auto-funnels and warm-ups). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "mailings/<item>"` (or `"mailings"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** posts on networks → `social`; who the customers are → `customers`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
