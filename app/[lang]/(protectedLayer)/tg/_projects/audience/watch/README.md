# Watchtower (Дозорный) — `projects/watch`

News and trends of your niche while they are fresh.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `cnn` (CNN), `trends` (Niche trends and news). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "watch/<item>"` (or `"watch"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** competitors' products → `competitors`; AI news → `ai`; your own ideas → `ideas`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
