# Brand and packaging (Бренд и упаковка) — `projects/brand`

How people recognise you and how you sound.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `positioning` (Positioning), `identity` (Visual identity), `tov` (Tone of voice), `personal` (Personal brand). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "brand/<item>"` (or `"brand"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** single posts and media → `content`; ads → `marketing`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
