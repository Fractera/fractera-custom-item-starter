# Community and networking (Комьюнити и нетворкинг) — `projects/community`

The people around you: clubs, contacts, partners.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `clubs` (Clubs and chats), `contacts` (Useful contacts), `affiliate` (Affiliate programs), `experts` (Experts). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "community/<item>"` (or `"community"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** customers → `customers`; your team → `team`; family and friends → `life/family`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
