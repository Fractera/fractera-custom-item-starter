# Marketing and traffic (Маркетинг и трафик) — `projects/marketing`

Where people come from and what it costs.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `ads` (Paid ads), `collabs` (Collaborations and bloggers), `promos` (Promotions and special offers), `seo` (Organic and SEO). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "marketing/<item>"` (or `"marketing"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** organic posts → `social`; the brand itself → `brand`; the sales funnel after the click → `sales`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
