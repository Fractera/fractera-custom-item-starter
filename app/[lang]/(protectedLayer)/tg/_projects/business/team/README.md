# Team and contractors (Команда и подрядчики) — `projects/team`

Who does the work with you.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `freelancers` (Freelancers), `partners` (Partners), `roles` (Areas of responsibility), `hiring` (Search and hiring). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "team/<item>"` (or `"team"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** staff on payroll → `hr`; AI staff → `digital-staff`; suppliers of goods → `suppliers`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
