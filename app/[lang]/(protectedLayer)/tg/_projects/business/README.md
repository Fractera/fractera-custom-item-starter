# Business (Дело) — a section of Projects

A STATIC folder: its groups below are folders; a new group = a folder with meta.json, README.md and items.json, then `npm run tg:projects` and a build.

- `product` — Product and services: What you sell, what it costs and where it grows.
- `my-services` — My services: The services you give people, from the request to the result.
- `sales` — Sales: The catalog and the client's way from interest to payment.
- `customers` — Customers: Who buys from you and how to keep each of them.
- `suppliers` — Suppliers: Where the goods come from and on what terms.
- `production` — Production: How your product is made: equipment, materials, quality.
- `subscriptions` — Subscriptions: The subscriptions you sell to your clients.
- `competitors` — Competitors: What others do and how you are better.
- `economy` — Economy: The money of the business: income, costs, budget and taxes.
- `investors` — Investors: Everything to show those who will invest.
- `fintech` — Fintech and payments: Accounts, taking payments, transfers and their safety.
- `legal` — Legal and documents: Contracts, taxes, rights and licences.
- `assets` — Assets: What you own: property, shares, brands, digital assets.
- `team` — Team and contractors: Who does the work with you.
- `hr` — Staff and HR: Staff, schedules, pay and growth of people.
- `digital-staff` — Digital staff: AI staff that work for you around the clock.
