# Legal and documents (Юридическое и документы) — `projects/legal`

Contracts, taxes, rights and licences.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `contracts` (Contracts and offer), `taxes` (Taxes and reporting), `copyright` (Copyright), `licences` (Licences and permits). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "legal/<item>"` (or `"legal"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** the money of taxes → `economy`; what you own → `assets`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
