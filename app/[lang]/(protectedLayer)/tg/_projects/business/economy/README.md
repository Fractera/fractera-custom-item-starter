# Economy (Экономика) — `projects/economy`

The money of the business: income, costs, budget and taxes.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `income` (Income and costs), `budget` (Budget and taxes). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "economy/<item>"` (or `"economy"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** your personal money → `life/finance`; payment rails and accounts → `fintech`; taxes as documents → `legal`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
