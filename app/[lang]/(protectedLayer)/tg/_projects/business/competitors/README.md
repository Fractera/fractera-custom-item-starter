# Competitors (Конкуренты) — `projects/competitors`

What others do and how you are better.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `product-research` (Product research), `financial-research` (Financial research), `offers` (Marketing and offers). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "competitors/<item>"` (or `"competitors"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** your own numbers → `economy` or `analytics`; trends of the niche → `watch`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
