# Analytics and hypotheses (Аналитика и гипотезы) — `projects/analytics`

Check ideas with numbers, not with feelings.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `tests` (Test ideas), `ab` (A/B experiments), `dashboards` (Dashboards and reports), `insights` (Conclusions and insights). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "analytics/<item>"` (or `"analytics"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** numbers for investors → `investors`; the budget → `economy`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
