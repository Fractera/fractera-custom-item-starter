# Career and study (Карьера и учёба) — `projects/career`

Your growth: study, portfolio, the next step.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `cv` (CV and portfolio), `jobs` (Job search and interviews), `study` (University, courses and exams), `grades` (Growth plan and grades). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "career/<item>"` (or `"career"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** active applications and interviews → `job-search`; general learning → `life/self`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
