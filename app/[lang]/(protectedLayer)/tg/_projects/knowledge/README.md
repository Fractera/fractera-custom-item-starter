# Knowledge and tools (Знания и инструменты) — a section of Projects

A STATIC folder: its groups below are folders; a new group = a folder with meta.json, README.md and items.json, then `npm run tg:projects` and a build.

- `ideas` — Ideas: The place where ideas are not lost.
- `focus` — Tasks and focus: Goals, tasks and the rhythm that moves them.
- `analytics` — Analytics and hypotheses: Check ideas with numbers, not with feelings.
- `knowledge-base` — Knowledge base: Everything you read, collected and want to find again.
- `tools` — Tools and services: The software, clouds and access the work rests on.
- `automation` — AI tools and automation: Tools and links that work for you.
- `ai` — Artificial intelligence: Models, agents and everything new in AI.
- `web3` — Blockchain and Web3: Wallets, assets, contracts and Web3 communities.
- `career` — Career and study: Your growth: study, portfolio, the next step.
- `job-search` — Job search: Applications, interviews and offers in one place.
