# Tools and services (Инструменты и сервисы) — `projects/tools`

The software, clouds and access the work rests on.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `software` (Software), `cloud` (Clouds and storage), `domains` (Domains and hosting), `access` (Access and passwords). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "tools/<item>"` (or `"tools"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** AI tools and bots → `automation`; subscriptions you sell → `subscriptions`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
