# AI tools and automation (Нейросети и автоматизация) — `projects/automation`

Tools and links that work for you.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `ai-tools` (AI tools), `prompts` (Prompt library), `bots` (Chat bots and scripts), `integrations` (Service integrations). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "automation/<item>"` (or `"automation"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** models and agents as a subject → `ai`; AI staff with a job → `digital-staff`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
