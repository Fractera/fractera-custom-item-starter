# Ideas (Идеи) — `projects/ideas`

The place where ideas are not lost.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `business` (Business), `personal` (Content and personal projects). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "ideas/<item>"` (or `"ideas"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** tasks with a date → `focus`; checked hypotheses → `analytics`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
