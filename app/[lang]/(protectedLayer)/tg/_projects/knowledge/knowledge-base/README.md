# Knowledge base (База знаний) — `projects/knowledge-base`

Everything you read, collected and want to find again.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `references` (References and inspiration), `books` (Books and courses), `guides` (Guides and checklists), `archive` (Archive). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "knowledge-base/<item>"` (or `"knowledge-base"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** news while fresh → `watch`; study plans → `career`; prompts → `automation`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
