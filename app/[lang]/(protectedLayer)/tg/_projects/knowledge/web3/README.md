# Blockchain and Web3 (Блокчейн и Web3) — `projects/web3`

Wallets, assets, contracts and Web3 communities.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `wallets` (Wallets, exchanges and key storage), `defi` (Portfolio, staking and DeFi), `contracts` (Smart contracts, tokenomics and launches), `nft` (NFT, DAO and Web3 communities), `airdrops` (Testnets, airdrops and on-chain analytics), `networks` (Networks, fees and bridges). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "web3/<item>"` (or `"web3"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** fiat accounts and acquiring → `fintech`; personal investing → `life/finance`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
