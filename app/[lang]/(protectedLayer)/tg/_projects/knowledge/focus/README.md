# Tasks and focus (Задачи и фокус) — `projects/focus`

Goals, tasks and the rhythm that moves them.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `goals` (Goals for the month / year), `current` (Current tasks), `routine` (Routine), `sprints` (Sprints and deadlines). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "focus/<item>"` (or `"focus"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** loose ideas → `ideas`; recurring jobs on a clock → the calendar (Cron) with `project`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
