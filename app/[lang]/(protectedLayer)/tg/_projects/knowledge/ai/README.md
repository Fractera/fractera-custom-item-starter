# Artificial intelligence (Искусственный интеллект) — `projects/ai`

Models, agents and everything new in AI.

- **Put here:** work of this direction — `llm` (Chats and language models), `system-prompts` (Prompts and system instructions), `generation` (Images, video and sound generation), `agents` (AI agents and automation scenarios), `rag` (Knowledge bases and model training (RAG)), `news` (News, reviews and AI selections). A page of the site or of «Администрирование» joins it with `"project": "ai/<item>"` (or `"ai"`) in its meta.json; a calendar entry with the same `project`.
- **Not here:** ready AI tools you use → `automation`; AI workers of the business → `digital-staff`.
- **A new item** (the person's «добавить», e.g. a new network): one entry in `items.json` — `slug`, `title` and `line` in en and ru. No build: the Mini App shows it within 30 s.
